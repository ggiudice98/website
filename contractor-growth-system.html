Contractor Growth System.

A full marketing system that turns your job sites into a pipeline — content, ads, and automations that catch every lead it brings in.

● 001 — The Problem

Challenges you are facing.

The work speaks for itself, but nobody sees it.

The quality is there. The finished projects are there. None of it exists anywhere a prospective client would look.

Everything comes from referrals.

Referrals are the best work you get. They're also the work you can't control. When they slow down, there's no second channel to turn on.

The same handful of relationships.

Relying on the same clients, if they're not calling, nobody is.

Leads go cold before anyone follows up.

Work you could've earned, lost to nothing more than timing.

● 002 — What it delivers

What the Contractor Growth System delivers.

An online presence foundation.

Positioning that clearly defines who you are and who you serve, built around the customers you want to win.

Project Showcases

We turn your active sites into content that highlights your expertise. Published consistently, so people see the quality before they ever call.

Ads built from what already performing.

Content runs organically first. Whatever performs gets ad spend behind it, whatever doesn't gets cut. You're not paying to test ideas, you're paying to scale the ones that already worked.

A CRM that captures every inquiry.

Each one logged and tagged by source, so you can see which jobs came from where and what's actually generating work.

Automated response and follow-up.

Inquiries get an immediate reply, and a follow-up sequence keeps running until the prospect books or answers. Email and text are handled automatically, so you're not chasing a lead between jobs.

Monthly Reports

Each month, you see what's working and what it costs to win a job. Campaigns are number-backed, so we're able to pivot effectively according to whats working.

● 003 — The program

How the program runs.

  1. Month one

    Foundation

    Positioning, branding and messaging. CRM and pipeline built. Ad accounts and tracking set up. Foundation content gets put out.

  2. Month two

    Visibility

    Content publishing at full cadence. We watch what performs organically. First campaigns launch, and responses start routing into the CRM.

  3. Month three

    Paid

    Budget moves behind what performed. Weak creative retired, audiences narrowed around who's responding.

  4. Month four

    Proof

    Campaigns optimised against conversion data. Full report at month end: what came in, what it cost, what closed.


Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will I see results?+

Content starts publishing in month one and ads go live in month two, so there's activity early. The number that matters — what an inquiry actually costs you — comes at the end of month four. Anyone promising that sooner is guessing.

What kind of ad budget do I need?+

The amount is set by you, but there's a floor below which campaigns can't gather enough data to optimize. We'll give you a realistic number on the call based on your market and who you're targeting.

Why four months?+

Month one you have nothing to advertise with. Month two you find out what people respond to. Month three you spend behind it. Month four you get the numbers. Shortcut any of those and you're spending on guesses.

What if I've worked with an agency before and it didn't go anywhere?+

Usually one of two things: they ran ads with no content behind them, or they made content nobody put budget behind. This does both, with your own footage instead of stock. And month four is a real checkpoint — you see the numbers and decide from there.
